ABOUT ME

Most recently I was a Help Desk Analyst on contract with UPMC, providing Tier 1 support through an enterprise Epic EHR go-live. That meant high call volume from clinical and administrative staff, fast triage of access and application issues, and escalations documented well enough for the next team to act on without a second call. The contract ended in October 2026 as go-live call volume tapered off.

Before that I spent a year and a half at IntermixIT, a managed service provider, as an Account Manager and the primary point of contact for roughly 70 client accounts and about 50 tickets a month. I owned the ticket lifecycle in Autotask PSA, worked alongside the engineers who handled escalations, and lived in Microsoft 365 every day — which is what pushed me to get MS-900.

On my own time I've been learning how automation and scripting fit into IT work — following tutorials and courses through Power Automate, Python, and Azure AI, then rebuilding the ideas as small projects so they actually stick. I'm still early on this and don't claim otherwise; it's a direction I'm growing into, not something I'd sell myself as an expert in yet.

I passed CompTIA A+ Core 1 in July 2026 and my Core 2 exam is scheduled. After that comes Windows administration, Active Directory, and Intune. I'm looking for a permanent help desk role where I can grow past Tier 1.
